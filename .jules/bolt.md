## 2024-05-15 - Recharts Dynamic Array References Cause Full Layout Thrashing
**Learning:** Recharts components (like `<BarChart>`) are highly sensitive to referential equality of the `data` prop. Passing dynamically generated arrays or objects directly into Recharts components causes expensive, unnecessary full SVG recalculations and layout thrashing on every render, which can severely impact frontend performance. Additionally, geometric props (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) passed to custom Recharts `<Label>` functions can be strings or undefined and must be safely coerced to numbers to prevent type issues or NaN arithmetic errors.
**Action:** Always wrap dynamically generated arrays or objects passed to Recharts components in `useMemo`. Always safely coerce geometric properties (`Number(x) || 0`) in custom `<Label>` or `<Shape>` components.
