## 2024-05-14 - Recharts Data Memoization & Strict Props
**Learning:** Recharts triggers expensive, full SVG recalculations and layout thrashing if the `data` arrays or objects passed to chart components (like `<BarChart data={...}>`) are recreated on every render. Additionally, geometric props (like `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) passed to custom labels or shapes in Recharts can be `undefined` or `string` values under the hood, leading to strict TypeScript arithmetic errors.
**Action:** Always wrap dynamically generated chart data arrays or objects in `useMemo` when rendering Recharts components. When implementing custom labels or shapes (like `<Label content={...} />`), ensure explicit `undefined` checks and `Number()` coercion for geometric props to satisfy strict TypeScript validation.
