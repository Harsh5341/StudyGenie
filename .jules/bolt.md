## 2025-02-23 - [Recharts Reference Invalidation and NaN Geometry Validation]
**Learning:** Recharts inherently recalculates expansive SVGs entirely if a new object/array data reference is provided upon every render. Furthermore, when creating custom `<Label>` or shape renderers, strict TS execution and math operations can fail silently or yield `NaN` layout errors when geometric coordinates (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) are sporadically `undefined` during specific Recharts inner lifecycle ticks.
**Action:** Always memoize any dynamically-generated arrays passed to `<BarChart data={...}>` to short-circuit re-renders. Always include explicit guard clauses for geometric props (`if (x === undefined || y === undefined || width === undefined || height === undefined) return null;`) when overriding Recharts label/content renders.
